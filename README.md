# Palmera Heat — La Paloma

A complete open-world crime game that plays offline in a desktop browser, built for make.fun's [Make GTA 6 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01).

Palmera City is being sold to Azure Crown Resorts — unless the heritage clause stops it. Play Rio, a courier for Mama Sol, and carry the city's hidden heritage pieces through four story missions: a lighthouse lens, a stolen '69 Solana Cabrio, a lighthouse keeper on the run, and the founding ferry bell La Paloma, all under the eye of Inspector Vega.

In the game: a full city on foot and by car, lane-following traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal, a 0–5 star wanted system with pursuing police and a BUSTED meter, melee and pistol combat, taxi fares with local gossip, a HUD with a live minimap, synthesized sound, and a localStorage save with Continue.

Run it: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080. Pure static files under `game/` — no build step, no network. Handed in by `main.ts` (Node 22), which copies `game/` and `screenshots/` to the output and writes the dossier with the kit's `vendor/makefun-game.ts`.
